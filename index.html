Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bacara is a local-first PDF reading, annotation, and knowledge workspace developed by Bacara Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how Bacara handles information and files when you use the application.

1. Local-First by Default

Bacara is designed to keep your reading data on your device by default. Imported PDF files, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, annotations, and local profile information are stored locally unless a future online feature is explicitly introduced and enabled.

2. PDF Files

PDF files that you import into Bacara are used to provide reading and annotation features. Bacara does not upload your PDF files to a remote server by default.

3. Reading and Annotation Data

Bacara may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Reading progress and last-read position
  • Bookmarks
  • Highlights
  • Notes
  • Pen, line, text, sticker, and other annotation data
  • Library metadata needed to organize imported books

4. Profile Information

If you create a Bacara profile, information such as your display name, username, bio, and profile photo is stored locally on your device in the current version of the app.

5. File and Photo Access

Bacara accesses files or images only when you choose to perform an action such as importing a PDF or selecting a profile photo. This access is used only to provide the requested app functionality.

6. Accounts and Sign-In

The current version of Bacara does not require a user account or sign-in to read PDFs and use the core local features.

7. Advertising

Bacara does not display third-party advertising in the current version of the app.

8. Analytics and Remote Data Collection

Bacara does not currently use a remote analytics backend to collect your reading activity, imported PDF content, highlights, notes, or annotations.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or other online services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy and the corresponding Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before those features are made available.

9. Subscriptions and Payments

Bacara may offer paid subscription features in the future. When enabled, payments made through Google Play will be processed by Google Play. Bacara will not store your full payment card details. This policy will be updated if additional payment-related data handling is introduced.

10. Sharing and Sale of Personal Data

Bacara does not sell your personal data. In the current local-first version, Bacara does not share your imported PDF content, highlights, notes, or annotations with third parties by default.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Bacara data remains on your device until you delete it through available in-app controls, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored Bacara data from that device.

12. Children

Bacara is not specifically directed to children. If the target audience or product behavior changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

13. Security

We aim to minimize privacy risk by keeping core reading data local by default and by limiting access to files and images to user-initiated actions. No method of storage or software system can guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bacara adds new features, online services, analytics, cloud synchronization, subscriptions, or other changes that affect how data is handled. The updated policy will include a revised “Last updated” date.
